THE ISSUE

What is gender-based online harassment?

Online spaces should allow people to communicate, learn, and participate without being targeted because of their sex or gender.

◈

In simple words

Gender-based online sexual harassment involves unwanted sexual or sexist behavior carried out through digital technologies. Under the Philippine Safe Spaces Act, covered acts can include unwanted sexual or sexist remarks, threats, cyberstalking, incessant messaging, unauthorized sharing of photos or videos, impersonation, and harmful false posts.

Source: Philippine Commission on Women
💬

Sexist remarks

Unwanted comments or messages based on a person's sex or gender.

📩

Incessant messaging

Repeated unwanted messages that disturb, intimidate, or harass.

📸

Sharing without consent

Sharing intimate or sexual content of another person without permission.

👤

Impersonation

Using another person's identity online to cause harm or harassment.

🔎

Cyberstalking

Repeated electronic communication or monitoring intended to intimidate.

⚠

Online threats

Threatening or intimidating someone through digital communication.

KNOW YOUR RIGHTS

Republic Act No. 11313

The Safe Spaces Act, also known as the Bawal Bastos Law, addresses gender-based sexual harassment in streets, public spaces, workplaces, educational institutions, and online spaces.

RA 11313

Safe Spaces Act

For online gender-based sexual harassment, the law identifies acts such as unwanted sexual or sexist remarks, threats, cyberstalking, incessant messaging, unauthorized sharing of photos or videos, impersonation, and harmful false posts.

01

Save evidence

Keep screenshots, links, usernames, dates, and other relevant records.

02

Use platform tools

Block, restrict, mute, or report harmful accounts and content when appropriate.

03

Seek support

Talk to a trusted person, school office, organization, or appropriate authority.

04

Know reporting channels

PCW identifies the NBI Cybercrime Division, PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group, and DOJ Office of Cybercrime as reporting channels for covered online harassment.
